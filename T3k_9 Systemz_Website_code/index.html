About

Everett Pulskamp

I'm an engineering student at Capistrano Valley High School based in Laguna Niguel, CA, building under the name T3K_9 Systemz. I care about anything with a circuit board or a chassis — electronics, PCB design, robotics programming, and manufacturing.

Most of my hands-on work happens through FRC Team 9271, the Cyber Porpoises, where I focus on the electrical and manufacturing side of the robot. Outside of competition season, I build my own tools — like the MakoPad, a macropad designed for teammates doing FRC programming — and take on solo projects to keep learning.
